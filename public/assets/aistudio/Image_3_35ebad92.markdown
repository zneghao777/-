## Brand & Style

This design system elevates intelligent computing into an immersive, flagship digital experience. It pairs deep obsidian-midnight backgrounds with the vivid luminescence of electric aurora teal and neon cyan. The aesthetic bridges high-performance dark minimalism with sophisticated glassmorphismâ€”featuring sub-pixel frost boundaries, ambient luminous backdrops, and refined typographic rhythm.

Designed for modern professionals, enterprise operators, and creators working with next-generation AI workflows, the UI instills quiet confidence, hyper-responsiveness, and technological precision. Every element prioritizes clarity under complex workflows while maintaining an aura of ethereal fluidity and high-end craft.

## Colors

The color architecture is calibrated around high-contrast illumination on deep abyss surfaces:

- **Primary (`#08BDBA`)**: Electric Aurora Cyan. Serves as the principal beacon for interactive triggers, progressive progress, primary actions, and focused states.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Midnight Slate. Powers elevated structural containers, toolbars, and contextual cards.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8`)**: Radiant Sky Cyan. Utilized for micro-badges, gradient transitions, streaming tokens, and contextual AI synthesis cues.
- **Neutral (`#090D16`)**: Pure Void Black. The base canvas tone designed to reduce visual fatigue while maximizing contrast.

### State & Feedback Values
- **Success**: `#10B981` (Emerald Glow)
- **Warning**: `#F59E0B` (Amber Radiant)
- **Destructive / Error**: `#F43F5E` (Crimson Pulse)
- **Surface Outline / Border**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on default, brightening to `rgba(8, 189, 186, 0.4)` on interactive focus.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric authority of `Sora` for titles and display nodes with the clean legibility of `Manrope` for dense conversational flows and system status indicators.

- **Headlines & Metric Callouts**: Sora delivers crisp, contemporary structure with wide counters and technical geometry, ideal for hero headers, AI prompt labels, and data points.
- **Body & Reading Content**: Manrope offers neutral, open apertures and robust x-height to maximize reading endurance across low-light settings and multi-turn message threads.
- **Monospace Syntax**: JetBrains Mono is deployed for code outputs, token streams, and configuration payloads.
